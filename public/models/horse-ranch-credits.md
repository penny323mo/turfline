# Textured ranch horse

**Lyndon Daniels** created the horse mesh and textures for the *Realtime Ranchers 3D Model Pack*. **ChadM** rigged the horse and released the Blender file on OpenGameArt. Both contributions are available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- [Rigged Horse and original Blender download](https://opengameart.org/content/rigged-horse)
- [Realtime Ranchers source pack](https://opengameart.org/content/realtime-ranchers-3d-model-pack)
- [Quaternius Horse and CC0 motion source](https://poly.pizza/m/qvTrSG9pZF)

The original Blender rig has no animation clips. This adaptation restores the packed 2K coat, hair and normal maps as glTF materials, weights the mane, tail and eyes, adds four fetlock joints, and retargets Quaternius's idle and gallop motion. The finished equine mesh has 37,160 triangles. The ranch horse has a stockier build and longer mane than a thoroughbred.

`horse-ranch.glb` contains the horse, two animation clips and all textures. The runtime adds the project's original jockey, racing silks, bridle, racecloth and stirrups.

`horse-ranch-mounted.glb` includes the same horse, the red-silk number 1 rider, and baked rein and rider motion in both clips. Both GLBs are self-contained.
